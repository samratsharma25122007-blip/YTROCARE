Why timely servicing matters

What happens when servicing is delayed

An RO purifier left unserviced beyond five to six months gradually loses effectiveness. These are the most common consequences.

Bacteria & biofilm

A saturated filter and stagnant storage tank allow bacteria and biofilm to accumulate before the final glass.

Rising TDS

A worn membrane stops rejecting dissolved salts and heavy metals, allowing TDS levels to climb.

Scaling & clogging

Hard-water scale restricts the membrane and pump, reducing flow rate and shortening equipment life.

Taste & odour

Exhausted carbon filters no longer remove chlorine and organic compounds, affecting taste and odour.

Frequently asked questions

How often should an RO purifier be serviced?

Every 5–6 months. Filters clog and membranes wear with use — past that window, TDS creeps up and bacteria can build in the tank, even if the water still looks clear.

How much does a service cost?

It depends on what your purifier needs. We diagnose first and give you an upfront quote for parts and labour — no work starts without your approval, and there are no hidden charges.

Which brands do you service?

All major brands — Kent, Aquaguard, Livpure, Pureit, Havells, Blue Star and more — plus assembled and commercial units. Our technicians carry brand-compatible genuine spares.

How quickly can a technician visit?

Usually the same day. Our average response time is around 60 minutes in core service areas; we confirm your slot on the phone right after you book.

Do you handle commercial and industrial RO plants?

Yes — installation, preventive maintenance, repair and AMC for plants from 250 LPH to 1000+ LPH used by schools, hospitals, hotels and factories.
